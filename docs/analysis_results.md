# Analysis Insights & Results

## Surge Multiplier vs Cancellations

- Cancellation rates increase from low/medium surge (~19–20%) to high surge (~21.7%)
- Very high surge shows a slight decrease (~20.5%), suggesting a threshold or selection effect
- Overall, higher surge correlates with higher cancellation risk, but extreme surge does not worsen cancellations proportionally

## Incentive Impact on Completion

- Completion rates are nearly identical for incentivized vs non-incentivized trips (~79%)
- Incentives do not materially improve completion rate in this dataset
- Suggests incentives may affect demand generation rather than operational execution

## Experiment: Upfront ETA Transparency

- Treatment conversion rate: ~53.1% vs Control: ~43.9%
- Absolute lift: ~+9.2 percentage points
- Interpretation: Showing ETA upfront improves conversion in this dataset, suggesting reduced uncertainty increases user commitment

## City Tier Performance

- Tier 1 and Tier 2 cities show similar completion rates (~80%)
- User cancellations dominate in both tiers
- No major operational degradation observed in Tier 2 cities

## Product-Level Outcomes

- Completion rates are consistent across ride-hailing, scooters, and e-bikes
- User cancellations exceed driver cancellations for all products
- No product shows clear operational underperformance

## Trip Funnel: Request → Pickup → Completion

- 100% of requested trips reach pickup, indicating no supply-side drop-off at matching
- Overall request-to-completion rate is ~79.5%
- All funnel attrition occurs post-pickup due to user or driver cancellations
- Suggests optimization efforts should focus on reducing cancellations rather than improving matching
